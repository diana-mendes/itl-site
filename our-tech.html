Our Tech

Designer
solvents,
tailored feedstock

Our approach combines designer solvents with AI-guided chemical discovery to create tailored feedstock from rubber scraps.

Two technology pillars

Pillar 01

Designer solvents

At the core of our technology are tunable solvent systems, whose properties can be adjusted by changing their components. They can achieve higher selectivity in the interaction with rubber, meaning that rubber properties such as elasticity and strength are better preserved.

Pillar 02

AI-guided discovery

There are millions of possible formulations, each resulting in different performance and outcome. We use predictive models to rank candidates before testing them in the labs. We're building proprietary datasets from our experimental process, combined with the large body of existing knowledge, which raise accuracy of our models over time.
